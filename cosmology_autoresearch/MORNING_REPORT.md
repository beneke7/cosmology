## What looks promising, and what is not

Promising is the *testing strategy*: the matched null exposes how ordinary the IVS profile gain is; the conditional holdout exposes that its apparent full-sample improvement does not travel uniformly across redshift; the independent early-time calculation shows that a tempting background pathology is not present over the tested range. Taken together, these are real constraints on the story even though none is a detection.

The low-effort parameter tweaks are not promising as standalone claims. Curvature's gain is routinely reproduced by the flat null. IVS's gain is common in its own matched null and fails the high-z predictive stress. CPL has the most interesting profile improvement among these screens, but its tail and domain sensitivity are insufficient to call it a result. More arbitrary BAO-only curve fitting is now lower value than testing whether a BAO-selected shape predicts a genuinely separate probe.
