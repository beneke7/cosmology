## Physical interpretation and negative results

The public BAO fit uses free α=c/(H0 rd), so it does not identify H0 or rd separately. Curvature is a standard-GR background departure in the profile, but the fit omits early-universe calibration, perturbations, massive neutrinos, CMB and growth. The nonzero fitted Ωk is therefore a best-fit coordinate, not a curvature measurement. The flat-null bootstrap demonstrates that such a small improvement is ordinary under this finite search.

The newest read papers do not form four independent confirmations: all share DESI DR2 BAO, while SN/CMB products have overlap or are alternative branches. In the interaction paper Q=Γρx, positive Γ means CDM→DE; in the metastable transfer models, positive Γ means DE→daughter sector. Those signs cannot be interchanged. The kinematic w(z) fits do not by themselves specify stable phantom-crossing perturbations. The MaVaN paper omits the relevant interacting perturbations and explicitly labels plotted off-diagonal correlations synthetic. These are physical limitations, not evidence that the alternatives are ruled out.

The exact Results IV Lyα geometry is not multiplied onto the old vector: that would double count the sample family, and the replacement covariance remains a genuine limitation. Its mock-biased fσ8 result is not used as evidence about growth or stability.
