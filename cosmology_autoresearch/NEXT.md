## Physical and data limits

- The fitted law is a flat, radiation-free, late-time background with Q=Γρx, w=−1, and separately conserved baryons. Positive Γ means CDM→vacuum in the paper convention; the fitted g is negative. The numerically checked positive baryon/CDM split is existential and uncalibrated and covers only 0≤z≤1.14418. Sampled m,x,E² positivity is not a continuous proof.
- This run does not evolve perturbations or establish CMB/BBN, growth, early-time, or microphysical viability. The exact author-modified CAMB patch/base commit, complete Cobaya settings and explicit initial conditions remain missing from checked public sources. If the user has them, request the original artifacts; otherwise any implementation remains independent rather than source-faithful.
- Keep DESI BAO and Dovekie as separate probes; no BAO×SN product or exact independence claim is justified by the missing cross-covariance. Do not add DESI Results IV Lyα to the current BAO vector without an official replacement likelihood and cross-covariance.
