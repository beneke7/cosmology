## Findings

**Paper/version trail.** The official [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05660) submission history displayed only v1 (submitted 2026-09-04 18:45:40 UTC) at access time; its version citation is `2609.05660v1`. The official [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05660) is also labelled v1. APS lists the same paper as [accepted for Physical Review D on 2026-09-03](https://journals.aps.org/prd/accepted/10.1103/6kdf-vzq4); that accepted-paper page says any author supplemental materials will be available upon publication of the version of record, and exposes no software artifact. Thus I found no post-v1 arXiv revision or later public software supplement on these primary sources as of the access time.

**What the paper does specify.** It defines `Q = Γ ρ_x`, with the conservation convention `dot(ρ_x)+3H(1+w_x)ρ_x=Q` and `dot(ρ_c)+3Hρ_c=-Q`; hence `Γ>0` transfers energy from CDM to DE and `Γ<0` the reverse. For IVS (`w_x=-1`) it gives synchronous-gauge CDM perturbation equations `δ'_c=−(θ_c+h'/2)+Γ a (ρ_x/ρ_c)δ_c` and `θ'_c=−ℋ θ_c`; the DE has no perturbative contribution. It states zero anisotropic stress and no momentum transfer in the DM rest frame. The table gives the IVS uniform `Γ/H0` range `[-3,3]`. It says a modified CAMB was used with Cobaya MCMC, Gelman–Rubin convergence diagnosis, and GetDist, but does **not** identify a CAMB fork, patch, release, commit, Cobaya/GetDist version, exact run YAML, or convergence threshold. Searches of the article text found no explicit adiabatic/regular perturbation initial-condition prescription or numerical initial values. These are paper-level omissions, not inferred defaults.

**Cited repository and version.** The paper cites [`williamgiare/wgcosmo`](https://github.com/williamgiare/wgcosmo) as reference [157], specifically in the paragraph describing the Cobaya interface used for MCEvidence; it does not cite it as the modified-CAMB source. ## Conclusion / unresolved items

The public primary-source trail does not establish the exact modified-CAMB patch/version, the complete Cobaya parameter/run configuration or dependency versions, the exact DES-Dovekie likelihood implementation/version, or the IVS perturbation initial conditions. The article's background sign convention, IVS perturbation equations, gauge/transfer assumptions, and broad coupling range are specified, but these do not identify the implementation. The existence of private, unlinked, or later-unpublished author code cannot be excluded by this audit.

**Gate recommendation:** do not treat this paper's reported MCMC results as an implementation-level validation target until those items—especially the CAMB patch and perturbation initialization—are recovered from the authors or an immutable artifact. A separately declared background-only profile can be run as its own model, but must not be presented as a reproduction of the paper's full perturbative analysis.
