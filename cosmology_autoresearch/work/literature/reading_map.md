# Literature and evidence map (first pass)

Access audit performed 2026-09-24. Local PDF hashes are SHA-256. “Full text” means the specified paper was read in full from a local PDF or complete arXiv HTML; it does not mean its numerical analysis was reproduced. Abstract-only items are explicitly separated. No novelty or reproduction claim is made here.

## Read-first implications

1. Keep the DR2 BAO mean/covariance likelihood as the common cosmology baseline. Its ruler is \(r_d\): the uncalibrated likelihood identifies \(H_0 r_d\), not \(H_0\) separately. Do not report an absolute Hubble constant without a stated early-universe calibration.
2. The collaboration's evolving-DE “sigma” values are profile-\(\Delta\chi^2_{\rm MAP}\) comparisons, conditional on data combination and model priors; they are not Bayesian odds or a stand-alone detection. The extended-DE paper is from the same collaboration/data pipeline, not an independent measurement.
3. The updated DES-Dovekie calibration changes the SN result materially. Analyze it as its own SN branch; do not multiply it by DES-SN5YR or assume independence from Pantheon+/Union3 low-z objects.
4. ACT DR6 spectra, ACT-lite, and P-ACT are alternate representations/combinations, not independent likelihood factors. In particular P-ACT already contains selected Planck spectra; the DR2 BAO paper's default CMB combination also includes Planck and ACT lensing.
5. DESI Lyα AP Results IV is a new full-shape analysis of the same DR2 Lyα spectra/quasar sample used for Lyα BAO Results I. Its paper constructs the updated DESI data set by replacing the Lyα BAO constraint with Lyα full shape and combining this with galaxy/quasar BAO. Do not multiply the Results I Lyα BAO row and the full-shape/AP likelihood unless a released joint covariance/likelihood explicitly permits it.
6. In the supplied thermodynamic-gravity paper, the constant-K field equation admits the exact positive-variable transform \(u=\exp(-K\phi)\), linearizing the prescribed-density stationary/time-dependent equation. This is a useful independent numerical route, not a novelty or stability result. The NGC 3198 fit is conditional: sphericalized baryons, a fitted mass-to-light ratio and K, and observed endpoint velocities imposed as boundary conditions.

## Priority reading cards

### DESI DR2 Results II: BAO and cosmological constraints

- Version and access: arXiv:2503.14738 v3, last revised 2025-10-09; local full PDF and extracted text read. Local PDF: context/papers/desi_dr2_bao.pdf, SHA-256 1e82f26e4cc3901b16168cd147f252bfa804f9c3caad3f4f7e3532640d237841. [arXiv version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14738).
- Governing relations: printed pp. 4–5, Eqs. (1)–(5) define the drag sound horizon and \(D_M/r_d,D_H/r_d\); Eq. (8) gives the evolving-DE density and Eqs. (9)–(10) define CPL \(w_0,w_a\). Printed p. 11, Eq. (12) defines isotropic and AP dilation combinations \(\alpha_{\rm iso}=(\alpha_\parallel\alpha_\perp)^{1/3}\), \(\alpha_{\rm AP}=\alpha_\parallel/\alpha_\perp\). These are the appropriate geometrical observables for the released compressed BAO vector.
- Measurement/likelihood: DR2 clustering analyses of BGS, LRG, LRG+ELG, ELG, QSO and Lyα samples; correlation-function multipoles use Landy–Szalay, RascalC covariance and marginalized broadband splines, with baseline \(s_{\min}=60\,h^{-1}{\rm Mpc}\) (Sec. III, printed pp. 9–16). The collaboration releases a 13-entry combined vector/covariance, with correlated \(D_M/r_d,D_H/r_d\) entries and a Lyα point at \(z_{\rm eff}=2.33\). The local mean/cov files are paired and pinned in sources.json to CobayaSampler/bao_data commit bb0c1c9009dc76d1391300e169e8df38fd1096db; local ordering must be preserved.
- Results and interpretation: printed Table VI, p. 24, reports CPL \(\Delta\chi^2_{\rm MAP}=-12.5\), nominal 3.1σ for DESI+CMB; with CMB+Pantheon+, Union3 and DESY5, 2.8σ, 3.8σ and 4.2σ. The paper explains that these are effective profile likelihood differences with two extra parameters, not Bayesian evidence. BAO-only constraints include \(H_0r_d=(101.54\pm0.73)\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) and \(\Omega_m=0.2975\pm0.0086\); BAO does not split \(H_0\) from \(r_d\) absent calibration (Sec. VII, printed pp. 23–25).
- Assumptions/limitations: broad late-time parameter domains; full-CMB baseline uses Planck low-\(\ell\), NPIPE PR4 CamSpec high-\(\ell\), and Planck+ACT lensing; an early-time compressed \((\theta_*,\omega_b,\omega_{bc})\) alternative is analyzed separately. Multiple SN compilations are alternative comparisons and have overlapping low-z data. Best-fit improvements are model/data/likelihood conditional and do not establish a physical DE mechanism.
- Code/data: [official DESI DR2 products](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/papers/dr2/); [chain and likelihood README](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/public/papers/y3/bao-cosmo-params/README.html); [pinned Gaussian BAO mean](https://github.com/CobayaSampler/bao_data/blob/bb0c1c9009dc76d1391300e169e8df38fd1096db/desi_bao_dr2/desi_gaussian_bao_ALL_GCcomb_mean.txt) and [covariance](https://github.com/CobayaSampler/bao_data/blob/bb0c1c9009dc76d1391300e169e8df38fd1096db/desi_bao_dr2/desi_gaussian_bao_ALL_GCcomb_cov.txt). Official README identifies the 13-point DR2 all-tracer set and default CMB combination.
- Experiment implication: run flat \(\Lambda\)CDM, constant-w, and CPL on the exact same vector/covariance with shared \(H_0 r_d\) convention and explicit priors. Treat published CMB+BAO values as a reference result, not as reproduced by a background-only profile fit.

### Extended dark energy with DESI DR2 BAO

- Version/access: arXiv:2503.14743 v2, last revised 2025-04-03; local full PDF/text read. context/papers/desi_extended_de.pdf, SHA-256 0de007414e3464ca00c81950a1db42c6be4a15dcac9bbabc559a7656e094274d. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14743).
- Locators: Table I, printed p. 5, supplies cosmological priors; CPL prior \(w_0\in[-3,1]\), \(w_a\in[-3,2]\), with \(w_0+w_a<0\) to enforce early matter domination. Section II and Table I define seven BAO samples including the Results I Lyα constraint. Equation (6), printed p. 7, gives the dark-energy density evolution; Sec. III, pp. 8–9, discusses phantom crossing and interpretation.
- Likelihood/data: Cobaya Metropolis–Hastings with modified CLASS/CAMB/PPF; DESI DR2 BAO, and separate Pantheon+, Union3, DESY5 branches; CMB either full Planck or compressed \((\theta_*,\omega_b,\omega_{bc})\). SN \(M_B\) marginalized analytically. It reports that a smooth fit to the distance data does not uniquely specify a microphysical scalar field; phantom crossing is phenomenological in this parameterization.
- Limitation/implication: valuable for reproducing collaboration prior/model conventions, but same collaboration and largely same data as Results II. Count as a methods/interpretation source, not independent corroboration.
- Data/code: shares official DR2 products above; no separate independent data product identified in the paper.

### DES-Dovekie recalibration and cosmology

- Version/access: arXiv:2511.07517 v3, last revised 2026-03-27; local full PDF/text read. context/papers/des_dovekie.pdf, SHA-256 cd3dd615c36afe0d45bcdfabd9c31611f0ca511e325997f93c3eb1f5a9744eb6. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07517).
- Locators: SN covariance \(\chi^2=\Delta\mu^\mathsf{T}C^{-1}\Delta\mu\), with systematic covariance assembled from systematic shifts and weights, Eqs. (8)–(9), printed pp. 4–5. Results are in Table 10 and model-comparison Table 11 (Sec. 7, printed pp. 19–20). Table 11 gives \(\Delta\chi^2_{\rm ML}=-13.5\), 3.2σ for flat CPL vs flat \(\Lambda\)CDM; \(\Delta\ln Z=-1.7\), described as weak preference for CPL (about 5:1 under the paper's convention). The paper explicitly cautions that the metrics answer different questions.
- Data/systematics: re-cross-calibrates DES-SN5YR and historical samples; fixes the F99 colour-law bug in simulations and calibration systematic weights, retrains SALT3.DOV, updates simulations/covariance. Updated sample has N=1820, including 1718 objects overlapping DES-SN5YR; do not count the original and recalibrated sample as independent. The SN likelihood marginalizes the absolute magnitude and cannot determine \(H_0\) alone.
- Main cosmology: DES-Dovekie+CMB+BAO flat CPL gives \(w_0=-0.803\pm0.054\), \(w_a=-0.72\pm0.21\). Its 3.2σ profile result is lower than the DR2+full-CMB+DESY5 4.2σ result in the earlier DR2 paper; versions/data treatments differ, so this is not a one-parameter controlled comparison.
- Code/data: [DES-SN5YR/Dovekie repository](https://github.com/des-science/DES-SN5YR) README marks the recalibration as superseding original results and releases distance vector/covariance, systematic components, likelihood and chains under release subdirectories; [documentation](https://des-sn-dr.readthedocs.io/en/latest/). The repository is mutable; pin a commit before analysis.
- Implication: Dovekie is the primary updated SN robustness branch and a calibration sensitivity result, not independent of legacy DES-SN5YR. Keep Pantheon+ and Union3 separate and state shared low-z sources.

### ACT DR6 power spectra / cosmological likelihoods

- Version/access: arXiv:2503.14452 v2, last revised 2025-06-24; local full PDF/text read. context/papers/act_dr6.pdf, SHA-256 4cbf9ccdb2ada7a07bd4bad878dc7f548bca1e963eeeb1b8694f77603a31b93e. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14452).
- Likelihood locators: Sec. 6.1 Eq. (17), printed p. 22, is the Gaussian likelihood for 1651 multifrequency TT/TE/EE bandpowers and the foreground model. Sec. 6.2 Eq. (33), printed p. 25, describes ACT-lite's 135 foreground-marginalized bandpowers and reduced calibration nuisance model. Sec. 6.3, printed pp. 26–27, defines Planck/ACT cuts in the P-ACT combination to mitigate overlapping sky/data.
- Main results: ACT DR6 power spectra support a well-fitting base-\(\Lambda\)CDM model. The paper reports P-ACT+ACT/Planck lensing+DESI DR2 (P-ACT-LB2) \(H_0=68.43\pm0.27\) under \(\Lambda\)CDM; P-ACT and DESI DR2 are reported as 1.6σ consistent. This is a CMB/BAO consistency check under a model, not evidence for evolving DE.
- Dependencies: ACT multifrequency spectra model foreground parameters (baseline uniform/Gaussian nuisances; Table 3) and calibration. P-ACT uses Planck PR3 and excludes overlapping high-\(\ell\) modes. The full ACT spectra, ACT-lite compressed likelihood, and P-ACT combination cannot be multiplied as separate factors. In addition, the DR2 paper's default CMB bundle already includes Planck plus ACT lensing; inspect exact likelihood provenance before combining it with an ACT-derived posterior.
- Code/data: [NASA LAMBDA DR6.02 products](https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/act/act_dr6.02/); [full MFLike implementation](https://github.com/ACTCollaboration/act_dr6_mflike); [ACT-lite](https://github.com/ACTCollaboration/DR6-ACT-lite); [lensing likelihood](https://github.com/ACTCollaboration/act_dr6_lenslike). LAMBDA exposes maps, spectra, covariance, chains and likelihood links.

### DESI DR2 Lyα AP / full-shape, Results IV

- Version/access: arXiv:2607.27410 v3, last revised 2026-08-04; local full PDF/text read. context/papers/desi_lya_ap.pdf, SHA-256 6fcc44addcd8843a46fc6016753955a5f16c7a3b2593c5c8e74250dc1c632674. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27410).
- Measurement/model: DESI DR2 Lyα auto- and Lyα–QSO cross-correlation full shape, from the same release/sample family as Lyα BAO Results I; AP anisotropy across broadband scales is modeled with nuisance treatment for RSD, continuum/small-scale distortions, UV-background scale-dependent bias, HCD absorbers, metals and quasar-redshift errors. Uses two mock suites, data splits and extensive variations; blinded baseline choices are reported.
- Locators/results: systematic treatment in Sec. V.A, printed pp. 18–20, Eqs. (20)–(26). Eq. (25) gives full-shape AP \(D_M/D_H(z_{\rm eff}=2.33)=4.572\pm0.046\) (1.0%). Eq. (26) combines this with isotropic BAO for \(D_M/r_d=39.32\pm0.33\), \(D_H/r_d=8.600\pm0.066\), correlation \(0.225\). Extended-model implications in Sec. VI.D, printed pp. 24–27 and Table IV: the paper reports CPL preference 2.7σ for its updated DESI+CMB combination and 3.2σ with DES-Dovekie; its DESI+CMB prediction is shifted modestly toward \(\Lambda\)CDM relative to prior DR2 BAO.
- Interpretation/limitations: authors report Lyα FS+galaxy/QSO BAO is consistent with lower-z BAO within flat \(\Lambda\)CDM and modestly reduces CMB discrepancy. Adding high-z data tightens the baseline but also introduces model dependence. The AP result is independent of \(r_d\), but the joint distances are not. Current paper states analysis data will be public with DR2 and figure points via Zenodo; a direct joint likelihood/covariance link was not identified in this first pass. Validation paper is a distinct companion, not independent survey data.
- Double-count contract: use the paper's updated “BAO+Lyα FS” combination, not the 13-row all-tracer DR2 Gaussian vector plus the same Lyα fullshape/AP result. The paper itself describes updated DESI as Lyα full shape plus galaxy/quasar BAO and compares against BAO combinations excluding the old Lyα point. Seek the exact released likelihood before merging it with a separate DR2 likelihood.
- Source trace: [Results I Lyα BAO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14739) v3 (2025-06-30) is the earlier compressed BAO analysis; [Results IV companion validation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27411) v2 (2026-08-04) validates full-shape model/systematics. Both are same-collaboration DR2 analyses.

### Supplied Pszota–Ván thermodynamic-gravity paper

- Version/access: user-supplied journal PDF, Phys. Dark Univ. 46 (2024) 101660, DOI [10.1016/j.dark.2024.101660](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dark.2024.101660); full 11-page PDF read, Eq. (1) visually checked. Local file context/user_provided/pszota_van_2024.pdf, SHA-256 55cbfbbb0a916ea2ce0b70523334ee301897695860fb4bdb5fd2278ab47b62e. Matching preprint arXiv:2306.01825 v3 (2024-09-20), full local PDF/text read: context/papers/tg_preprint.pdf, SHA-256 73befc859339a2aa4603e0e26b1f5fc1ddcf62ef0c98a8ef88c9e6718e8726af. Visually checked printed Eq. (1) in the supplied journal version; both preprint/journal versions are locally available, not asserted byte-identical.
- Governing equation and signs: Eq. (1), printed p. 2, \(\partial_t\phi=(\ell^2/\tau)(\nabla^2\phi-K|\nabla\phi|^2-4\pi G\rho)\). Spherical symmetry Eq. (2), p. 3; stationary Riccati equation and \(g=-\phi'\) sign convention are Eqs. (5)–(8), pp. 3–4. Vacuum branch Eqs. (9)–(17), pp. 4–5, has \(g=-1/(Kr+Cr^2)\); a singularity at \(r=0\) means it applies outside the baryonic bulk, and \(K\to0\) gives the Newtonian point-mass limit. The text explicitly notes an analytical comparison requiring the opposite K sign; reconcile sign conventions before comparing.
- Numerical/empirical assumptions: staggered finite-difference relaxational solve, Eqs. (34)–(42), pp. 7–8. For NGC 3198, the reported model fits \(K\) and \(\Upsilon_1\); source is stellar disk plus atomic hydrogen represented in spherical symmetry. Crucially, Eqs. (41)–(42) impose observed endpoint velocities at the innermost radius and at \(r_d\), the furthest measured radius. Therefore this is a conditional curve fit, not a held-out prediction or an isolated boundary-at-infinity test. Authors themselves caution that the spherical \(\Upsilon_1\) is not strictly physical and describe the work as proof of concept. Data availability says the galaxy inputs are from SPARC; paper gives no public analysis code.
- Mathematical experiment implication: for fixed \(\rho\), constant K and positive \(u=e^{-K\phi}\), the exact chain rule gives \(\partial_tu=D(\nabla^2u+4\pi G K\rho\,u)\), \(D=\ell^2/\tau\); stationarity is a linear elliptic equation. This is an algebraic transformation of their equation, not a novelty, complete solution, or stability proof. Derive transformed boundary data from the original conditions, test \(u>0\), and evaluate \(K\to0\) via the Poisson equation to avoid cancellation/division by K. It is not equivalent to coupled matter evolution.
- Data link: [SPARC archive](https://astroweb.case.edu/SPARC/) and local context/data/Rotmod_LTG.zip, sfb_LTG.zip. SPARC's rotation-curve components do not uniquely determine a 3D density; a spherical surrogate is a physical assumption, especially for gas/disk terms.

### SPARC source model

- Version/access: Lelli, McGaugh, Schombert & Pawlowski, “SPARC: Mass Models for 175 Disk Galaxies,” arXiv:1606.09251 v1 (2016-06-29); local full PDF/text read, context/papers/sparc_master.pdf, SHA-256 d089215877213661e40965543ee7e05736619082ad16d95e65ec059029588c63.
- Relevance: source of the velocity and baryonic-component profiles used in the supplied TG case. Dataset includes 175 nearby disk galaxies, 3.6μm surface photometry and curated rotation curves; mass models retain stellar mass-to-light assumptions and disk/bulge/gas geometries. The paper discusses disk–halo degeneracy and stellar \(\Upsilon_*\) uncertainty (Secs. 2, 5).
- Limit: tabulated radial acceleration/velocity components are not a unique spherical density profile. Geometry conversion and distance/inclination uncertainties must be explicit. [Public SPARC archive](https://astroweb.case.edu/SPARC/).

## Near-term likelihood and boundary-condition contract

- BAO-only cosmology: preserve the exact 13-vector ordering and full covariance; use \(\alpha=(H_0r_d)\) or equivalent explicit ruler amplitude. State redshift domain, neutrino/radiation treatment, and parameter bounds. Profile fit is screening, not official posterior reproduction.
- CMB extension: use one declared route only (full Planck/CamSpec + lensing bundle; P-ACT; ACT-lite; or compressed early-universe priors). Record included constituent datasets and shared lensing/spectra.
- Supernova extension: one SN compilation per branch. Profile/marginalize one absolute magnitude; never infer \(H_0\) from uncalibrated SN. State overlap and recalibration status.
- Lyα: either original DR2 all-tracer compressed BAO (including Lyα BAO) or released Results IV fullshape/AP joint product excluding/replacing that row. Do not multiply the representations.
- TG stationary solve: record source density geometry, \(K\) sign and units, \(D=\ell^2/\tau\), transformed boundary conditions and exterior solution. Distinguish three tasks: synthetic equation check, conditional reproduction with the paper's endpoint conditions, and a genuinely predictive isolated/held-out-galaxy test. The latter cannot use held-out endpoint velocities as boundary data.
