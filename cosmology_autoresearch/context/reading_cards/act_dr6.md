- Source/version: arXiv:2503.14452 v2; local full PDF/text read at `context/papers/act_dr6.pdf` (SHA-256 `4cbf9ccdb2ada7a07bd4bad878dc7f548bca1e963eeeb1b8694f77603a31b93e`).
- Question: what do ACT DR6 multifrequency power spectra and compressed alternatives imply for LCDM and combined cosmology?
- Likelihoods: Eq. (17), p. 22, describes 1,651 full-spectrum bandpowers; Eq. (33), p. 25, describes ACT-lite's 135 compressed bandpowers; Sec. 6.3, pp. 26–27, defines P-ACT Planck/ACT overlap cuts.
- Overlap: full ACT spectra, ACT-lite and P-ACT are alternatives, not independent factors. P-ACT contains selected Planck spectra; the DESI DR2 paper's default CMB combination includes Planck and ACT/Planck lensing.
- Limits: likelihood representation and foreground/calibration nuisance assumptions differ; combining published posteriors with their constituent spectra risks double-counting.
- Experiment implication: before a CMB extension, name exact data, covariances and shared spectra/lensing. The present campaign did not evaluate ACT likelihood products.
