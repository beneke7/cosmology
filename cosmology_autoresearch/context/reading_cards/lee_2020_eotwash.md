## Observable and result

The rotating-attractor torsion balance measured the 18ω and 120ω harmonics over 52 μm–3.0 mm. Newtonian gravity fit well; the paper reports no preferred Yukawa term at 2σ. Its familiar 95% `|α|` envelope crosses unit magnitude at λ≈38.6 μm, matching the headline “gravitational-strength Yukawa” summary.

The 2026 higher-gradient thermodynamic paper’s **one-length** point-source limit has Yukawa amplitude α=−1 and range λ=ℓ₁ when ℓ₂=0. Use the sign-specific column of thesis Table 11.4, not the sign-agnostic envelope: at λ=25 μm, −α₉₅=−1.27, so α=−1 is inside the reported interval; at λ=28 μm, −α₉₅=−0.886, so α=−1 is outside it. Straight interpolation of these rounded tabulated limits gives a one-length crossing near λ≈27.1 μm. This is a table-based profile read-off, **not** a new likelihood fit or a newly calibrated confidence interval. It is a stronger one-sided null than the 38.6 μm summary for α=−1.

## Scope and next test

This constrains the `ℓ₂=0` branch: approximately ℓ₁≲27 μm from the published sign-resolved curve. It does not by itself constrain all `(ℓ₁,ℓ₂)` values. In the real-root two-length model, the potential is a constrained sum of oppositely signed Yukawa terms; although its Fourier transfer is further suppressed relative to Newtonian gravity, the torsion torque is a geometry-weighted derivative. A defensible bound on the full pair requires the two-range torque prediction and a joint re-profile of the experiment’s geometric/calibration nuisances. The open thesis supplies analyzed torque rows and model details, so feasibility can be assessed without substituting the headline one-range limit.

The 2026 higher-gradient sector must also remain distinct from Pszota & Ván’s 2024 nonlinear galaxy `K` model: the follow-up sets its reactive coupling to zero for the stability/short-range analysis and provides no parameter mapping to the 2024 galactic fit.
