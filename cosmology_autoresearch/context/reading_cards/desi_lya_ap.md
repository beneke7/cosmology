# DESI DR2 Ly-alpha AP/full-shape — Results IV

- Source/version: arXiv:2607.27410 v3; local full PDF/text read at `context/papers/desi_lya_ap.pdf` (SHA-256 `6fcc44addcd8843a46fc6016753955a5f16c7a3b2593c5c8e74250dc1c632674`).
- Question: what does anisotropic full-shape fitting of DR2 Ly-alpha auto- and Ly-alpha–QSO correlations add to high-redshift geometry?
- Governing results: Eqs. (24)–(26), pp. 18–20; at z_eff=2.33, broadband AP gives DM/DH=4.578±0.052, combined full-shape AP gives 4.572±0.046, and the joint full-shape AP+BAO constraints are DM/rd=39.32±0.33 and DH/rd=8.600±0.066 with correlation +0.225. Systematic modeling is discussed in Sec. V; extended-model implications in Sec. VI.D.
- Data/overlap: this uses the same DR2 Ly-alpha sample family as Results I's earlier compressed Ly-alpha BAO measurement. The authors' updated BAO+Ly-alpha full-shape product replaces that older Ly-alpha representation rather than adding an independent point.
- Validation boundary: the companion [validation paper, arXiv:2607.27411 v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27411) reports successful BAO/AP checks across mock suites and splits, but a significant mock bias in fσ8; growth was excluded from the final analysis. It is the same collaboration/sample, not independent survey evidence. - Limits: a directly usable joint likelihood/covariance URL was not identified in the literature pass. The official DESI DR2 release page states spectra/redshift catalogs are not yet public; do not reconstruct a replacement likelihood from plotted points alone.
