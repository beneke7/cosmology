                                                                                    Supernovae data
                                                                                         Pin Wang, Ping Wang, and Jielei Zhang∗
                                                                        School of Physics and Electronics, Henan University, Kaifeng 475004, China

                                                                 Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) Collaboration reported an evidence for dynam-
                                                              ical dark energy model using baryon acoustic oscillation (BAO) measurements from DESI Data
                                                              Release 2 (DR2), cosmic microwave background (CMB), and three Type Ia Supernovae (SNe) data
                                                              (Pantheon+, Union3, and DES-SN5YR) recently. The significances are from 2.8 ∼ 4.2σ depending
                                                              on which SNe sample is used, and the highest significance is obtained based on the DES-SN5YR
                                                              data. But more recently, Dark Energy Survey (DES) Collaboration recalibrated the DES-SN5YR
                                                              data, and a significance of only 3.2σ is obtained based on the calibrated data, which is called
arXiv:2609.10567v1 [physics.gen-ph] 1 Sep 2026




                                                              DES-Dovekie. In this work, we perform a comprehensive analysis of five representative dynamical
                                                              dark energy parametrization models, including Chevallier-Polarski-Linder (CPL), Barboza-Alcaniz
                                                              (BA), exponential (EXP), logarithmic (LOG), and Jassal-Bagla-Padmanabhan (JBP), using DESI
                                                              DR2 BAO, Planck CMB, ACT DR6, and SNe data from Pantheon+, Union3, and DES-Dovekie.
                                                              Similarly, a significance of around 3σ is obtained based on the DES-Dovekie data. And the signif-
                                                              icances are 2.1 ∼ 3.7σ for different SNe samples and different parametrization models, indicating
                                                              an evolutionary dark energy dynamics. Furthermore, a phantom-like behavior in the past and has
                                                              transitioned into quintessence-like behavior today for the dynamical dark energy is preferred.
