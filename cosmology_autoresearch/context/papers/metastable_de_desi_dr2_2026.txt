                                                                We revisit metastable dark energy (DE) models described by a radioactive-like decay law. We
                                                             consider three scenarios: an effective, exponentially decaying DE component; decay of DE into non-
                                                             baryonic dark matter (DM); and decay of DE into dark radiation (DR). We constrain the metastable
                                                             DE models using DESI DR2 baryon acoustic oscillation (BAO) data, Type Ia supernovae (SNIa),
                                                             cosmic microwave background (CMB) observations, and, for the first time, the current available
                                                             DESI DR1 full-shape (FS) clustering measurements. The BAO+SNIa combinations show a mild
                                                             preference for positive Γ/H0 , with a deviation from the ΛCDM limit at the ≳ 2σ level. This
                                                             corresponds to a decaying DE density and an effective quintessence-like behaviour at low redshift.
                                                             Once CMB information from either Planck or P-ACT is included, however, the constraints become
                                                             statistically consistent with Γ/H0 = 0. The FS measurements probe the growth sector and help
                                                             distinguish the interacting DM-DE behaviour of Model 2 from the effective decaying-DE response
                                                             of Model 1 and the weaker DR-induced response of Model 3. For CMB+FS+DES-Dovekie, Model
arXiv:2608.01844v1 [astro-ph.CO] 3 Aug 2026




                                                             1 shows a slight deviation from Γ/H0 = 0 at the ≳ 2σ level, while Models 2 and 3 remain consistent
                                                             with the ΛCDM limit within 2σ. Overall, metastable DE remains phenomenologically viable: current
                                                             data allow late-time dynamics but do not provide decisive evidence for a nonzero decay rate. These
                                                             results motivate extending our analysis to the upcoming DESI DR2 FS data to obtain tighter
                                                             constraints on metastable dynamics.
