# Survey opportunities checked on 2026-09-23

For immediate cosmological model comparisons, start with published likelihoods and summary statistics. New images and catalogs are exciting for methods work, but are not automatically a released cosmology likelihood. The project ideas below are our assessments of fit to this research campaign.

| Resource | Current or planned status | Useful project |
|---|---|---|
| DESI | Public DR2 BAO products and chains; July 2026 Lyα Alcock–Paczyński/full-shape result. The main-survey raw-data index still lists DR1 as latest. | Reproduce BAO fits; test whether simple expansion changes survive the newer high-redshift information. Keep overlapping analyses correlated. |
| ACT | Public DR6.02 maps, spectra/covariances, chains and full/CMB-only likelihood links. | Add a demanding early-universe or lensing check once background baselines work. Avoid downloading all maps when a small likelihood suffices. |
| DES Y6 / KiDS-Legacy | Current full-survey lensing/clustering papers and released products; verify the exact likelihood and nuisance files needed. | Growth/geometry consistency and photometric-redshift, intrinsic-alignment or baryon-systematic robustness. Full reproduction can exceed one night. |
| SPHEREx | QR3 began September 2026. The mission schedule targets the first 102-channel all-sky cubes for November 2026. | Spectral-energy-distribution/redshift methods, calibration and cross-matching with optical surveys. Quick-release images are available now; mission-level clustering likelihood products are later. |
| Euclid | Q1 is available for astrophysical methods work; Q2 is a Galactic-bulge release. The updated DR1 schedule has Foundation in November 2026 and complete DR1 in mid-2027. | Prepare source-selection, cross-matching and lensing/clustering pipelines. Distinguish the first data tranche from the complete higher-level cosmology release. |
| Rubin | Early DP2 coadds/catalogs released 27 July 2026; remaining visit/difference images target October–December 2026. Annual DR1 is currently anticipated by end-June 2028. | Time-domain classification, image methods and early catalog validation. RSP access is tied to data rights; check eligibility. Alerts are accessible through brokers. |
| Roman | Launched 30 August 2026. NASA anticipates first images by early 2027. | Prepare simulations, selection models and joint-survey analyses. Launch and first images do not constitute a released dark-energy likelihood. |

Upcoming dates are targets and should be refreshed at the next run. Older pages still contain superseded Euclid and Roman schedules; use the current release/launch notices below.

The most interesting new-data side project for this machine is a narrowly defined cross-survey calibration or redshift experiment using SPHEREx and an optical reference sample, or a Euclid/Rubin methods benchmark. These are candidate directions, not demonstrated novel results. Selection functions, resolution/deblending differences and sample overlap must be modelled before making cosmological claims.
